# Xperts Hub — Idea Brainstorming

Brainstorm notes for the Digital Services OS (SaaS). These are ideas to evaluate, not commitments.

## Hiring & Careers

- **Career page section** served directly by Frappe (www page) — job posts render on the
  company's own domain; applicants apply inline.
- **Job Application** doctype: candidate name, email, phone, CV (file), message, status
  (New / Screening / Interview / Offer / Hired / Rejected), source (career page / LinkedIn /
  WhatsApp / referral), assigned recruiter.
- **LinkedIn integration**:
  - Post jobs to LinkedIn (company page) from the app
  - Pull job posts / candidate activity
  - "Easy Apply"-style capture
- **WhatsApp integration**:
  - Candidate sends CV + message to a company WhatsApp number
  - Auto-saved as a Job Application (CV file stored in Frappe)
  - Two-way chat to schedule interview, ask questions
- **Social Media Hub**: one inbox/outbox for LinkedIn + WhatsApp + email for hiring and marketing

## Productivity & Tracking

- Windows Tracker (desktop + browser) feeds real activity into:
  - Employee daily productive hours (9h model, breaks excluded — exists)
  - Project timesheets (auto from apps/domains per project)
  - Manager dashboards by department (exists in crm-analytics)
- Browser extension: Google Maps scraping → leads, competitor kiosks (exists in tracker repo)

## Automation

- Auto follow-up schedule → auto-assign → auto-dial → outcome (exists)
- AI drafting for client replies, job descriptions, CV screening
- Auto-billing from tracked hours + project milestones
- Telegram / Slack / WhatsApp notifications on lead changes, follow-up due, invoices, job applications

## Billing (Digital UOM)

- UOMs: Hourly, Sprint/Retainer, Project-Fixed, Deliverable
- Auto-build invoice from timesheets + milestones
- Simple payment tracking (ACH/Wire notes), not full accounting

## Community / Extras (later)

- Public company profile / portfolio page
- Client portal for approvals and invoice viewing
- Referral tracking for hiring
